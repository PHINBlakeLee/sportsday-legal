Silly Sports Day — Delete your account and data

Thank you for playing Silly Sports Day (developer: Blake). You can delete your account and related data as follows.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  2. Confirm the deletion when asked.
  3. If you cannot do this in the app, email us at the address below and include the Google account email you signed in with. We process requests within 7 business days.

What is deleted

DataHow it is handled
Google account emailDeleted immediately
NicknameDeleted immediately
Game records (scores and ranks), coins and itemsDeleted immediately
Group membershipDeleted immediately
Past ranking entries in the Hall of FameKept with the nickname replaced by "withdrawn player" (to preserve other users' ranking history)
Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)Deleted automatically after the retention period (analytics up to 14 months, crash reports 90 days). Signing out turns collection off and clears analytics data on your device.

Guest users

If you only played as a guest, no personal data is stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes the records stored on your device.
