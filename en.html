Silly Sports Day — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026

Silly Sports Day ("the app", Korean name: 엉망진창 운동회) is operated by the developer Blake ("we"). This page explains what we collect and why. South Korean law (the Personal Information Protection Act) is our baseline, and we also describe rights available to users in other regions.

We do not send your name, email, nickname or score values to analytics, and we do not collect the advertising ID, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data or payment data. Guest users (not signed in) have no personal data sent to our servers and no analytics are collected.

2. Minimum age

Signing in requires that you confirm you are at least 14 (South Korea and most regions), 15 (Japan) or 16 (EU/EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland), based on your device's region setting. Younger users can play as guests only.

3. Legal bases (EU/UK users)

Account and game data: performance of the service you request. Usage statistics and crash reports: your consent. Security and abuse prevention: our legitimate interests.

4. Sharing, processors and international transfers

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Because our servers are in South Korea, your data is transferred there (the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision for South Korea). For Google services, transfers rely on Google's own safeguards.

5. Retention

Account and game data are kept until you delete your account. Firebase Analytics data is kept up to 14 months and Crashlytics data 90 days (possibly shorter depending on our Firebase settings). After account deletion, past ranking entries in the Hall of Fame may remain with the nickname replaced by "withdrawn player".

6. Your rights

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or see the account deletion page. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, portability, restriction of processing, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time (EU/UK GDPR); request disclosure and deletion and opt out of sale or sharing (California CCPA/CPRA — we do not sell or share personal information); or request disclosure, correction and suspension of use (Japan APPI). Contact us at the address below. EU/UK users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

7. Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), sign-in uses OAuth without passwords, and access to the database is restricted.

8. Advertising

The app currently contains no advertising SDKs. If advertising is added, we will update this policy first.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
